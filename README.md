# Latria

**Flesh. Faith. Dominion.**

[简体中文](docs/README_zh-CN.md)

Empires rise beneath sacred banners. Dynasties endure through blood and marriage. Yet a secret behind a chamber door can threaten what generations have built.

**Latria** is a sexual and romantic expansion project for **Crusader Kings III**, exploring the struggle between desire, devotion, and power. Its vision reaches from the private lives of rulers to the fate of their houses, where love, forbidden pleasure, and religious scandal belong to the same history as succession, conquest, and the fall of crowns.

A sovereign may command armies and demand obedience. Neither the heart nor the body is so easily ruled.

## Desire and Dynasty

Marriage binds great houses, but duty does not settle every longing. Affection, loyalty, jealousy, and betrayal shape the lives behind the heraldry. Latria explores the tension between the person who bears a crown and the dynasty that expects them to endure, with intimate bonds carrying consequences for family, reputation, and posterity.

## Flesh and Spirit

The pleasures of the flesh offer solace, yet neither indulgence nor restraint is without a price. Desire, satisfaction, exhaustion, and frailty give rulers a life beyond their titles. The body that carries a dynasty can sustain its hopes or fail beneath them; the spirit that seeks peace can find relief or be consumed by its own demands.

## Faith and Judgment

Faith gives desire a language of virtue, sin, duty, and redemption. A private choice may conflict with a sacred vow, and the judgment of a community may differ from the conviction of its ruler. Across the medieval world's religious traditions, Latria explores how devotion and desire contend for the same soul, and how those entrusted to judge others face judgment themselves.

## Scandal and Sovereignty

What begins in secrecy can end before an entire realm. Intimacy offers trust and exposes weakness; scandal gives enemies a cause, and religious authority can turn accusation into a struggle over who has the right to rule. Latria carries these conflicts from chambers and courts to the confrontation between sacred authority and worldly crowns, where redemption and dominion can become rival names for the same ambition.

## An Expanding Chronicle

Latria is an ongoing project with a long-term vision: a richer medieval world in which desire, faith, and dynastic ambition are inseparable. Development will continue to deepen these connections, bringing new perspectives and stories to the lives of rulers and the realms they leave behind.
