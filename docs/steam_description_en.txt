[h1]Latria[/h1]

[i]No crown commands desire. No throne escapes judgment.[/i]

Your house has a name to defend, heirs to secure, and enemies waiting for a moment of weakness. Yet the sovereign beneath the crown has longings that duty cannot silence.

A lover's confidence can become a court's accusation. A broken vow can call sacred authority into question. What begins behind a closed door may end with armies beneath the walls.

[b]Latria[/b] is a sexual and romantic expansion for [b]Crusader Kings III[/b], bringing desire, devotion, and dominion into the story of your dynasty. Between the pleasures of the flesh and the claims of the soul, a ruler may find solace, salvation, or ruin.

[h2]The Heart Beneath the Crown[/h2]

Marriage serves your house. Love may ask something else of you. Spouses, lovers, loyalty, and betrayal give private life a place beside the ambitions of a dynasty. Those closest to you may become your refuge, your weakness, or the people who remember what the chroniclers leave unwritten.

[h2]The Price of Pleasure[/h2]

Your rank cannot spare you the demands of the body. Unanswered desire weighs on the spirit; indulgence can leave its own wounds. Satisfaction, restraint, and exhaustion make the pursuit of pleasure a part of a ruler's life, with consequences that do not vanish when the chamber door opens.

[h2]Before Gods and Princes[/h2]

Vows bind the devout, and scandal reaches even the highest seats of authority. Conscience, religious judgment, and the right to rule can collide. A faith that demands obedience may face the failings of its own leaders; a ruler accused of sin may answer with submission or defiance.

[h2]From Secrets to Sovereignty[/h2]

Intimacy can shelter an ambition or expose it. Secrets become leverage, accusations become causes, and struggles over virtue can grow into struggles over crowns. In Latria, the distance between private disgrace and a realm's upheaval may be shorter than any sovereign expects.

[h2]A Chronicle Still Unfolding[/h2]

Latria continues to grow around a long-term vision of a medieval world where desire, faith, and dynastic ambition shape one another. New stories and perspectives will carry that vision further.

Writers, translators, artists, scripters, and players are welcome to help shape what comes next. Share ideas, report problems, or contribute at the [url=https://github.com/czxieddan/Latria]GitHub repository[/url].
